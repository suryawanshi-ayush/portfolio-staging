About

Chemical engineer, catalysis-trained, heading for hydrogen

I started in core chemical engineering in Indore, specialised in catalysis at IIT Madras, did my synthesis work in Trondheim, and now work on effluent treatment at LOXIM Industries. Along the way I learned to run the whole loop: make the catalyst, build the electrode, test it honestly, and think about the process around it.
