Research and projects

Keeping platinum clean in formic acid fuel cells

IIT Madras, with synthesis at NTNU, Norway

Contribution. Made and characterised four Bi-modified Pt/C catalysts, tuned the catalyst ink, and tested them with and without a quinone mediator (AQSA) in a three-electrode half-cell.

Result. Bi(1.25)-Pt/C, with the least bismuth, retained 92.9% of its current over a one-hour hold, against 58.5% for commercial Pt/C. The mediator did not improve retention.

May 2025 – May 2026

Reworking a catalyst synthesis after an ignition on the filter

KinCat group, Department of Chemical Engineering, NTNU, Trondheim

Contribution. Ran the three batches and changed the route after each one: an aqueous borohydride step, less reductant and a changed filtration.

Result. No ignition was observed in Batch 3, which also gave the highest recovered mass, about 220 mg, with uniform SEM morphology.

Aug – Dec 2025

Oxygen reduction on a 13-atom platinum cluster

IIT Madras

Contribution. Individual course project: built, optimised and checked the Pt13 structures and the adsorbed intermediates in Gaussian 16 and tabulated the step energies.

Result. All four tabulated step energies are negative (−1.85 to −3.90 eV, electronic energies), and all optimised structures are true minima.

Jan – May 2025

Designing an SNCR retrofit for a biomass boiler

NTNU, Trondheim

Contribution. Member of a four-person team and presenter of the poster in Oslo. The report does not itemise who did what, so the method and results below are the team’s work.

Result. With a conservative design reduction of about 60% (the ideal model gave above 90%), modelled NOx falls to roughly 100–125 mg/Nm³, under the limit. Estimated capital cost is about 432 kUSD and running cost about 42 kUSD a year: not profitable at current NOx tax, but justified as a compliance cost.
